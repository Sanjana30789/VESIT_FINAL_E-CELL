E-SUMMIT'25

E-Summit at VESIT is a flagship event being organized as part of the symposium titled "Technology Advancements for Sustainability," conducted by VESIT to commemorate its Ruby Jubilee Year Celebrations. The symposium will take place from 13th to 16th February 2025, and the much-anticipated E-Summit is scheduled for 15th February 2025. We organize various events throughout the year, including workshops on idea generation, business planning, and funding, as well as competitions that allow students to showcase their startups and entrepreneurial potential.
